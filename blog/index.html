About Me

Hi, I'm Ben Boyette — I created this site.

Daily AI Briefing started as a way to automatically pull together AI news every morning instead of checking a dozen different sites by hand. This blog is where I'll write more informally: news commentary, and useful things I pick up while actually interacting with AI day to day.

One thing I want to be upfront about: I'm not an AI guru. I can't develop my own models, I don't own a device that can run the best open source models, I don't pay for a whole lot of AI products, and I don't even use a lot of AI applications beyond the models themselves. I'm just someone who's interested in this space and writes down what I notice.
